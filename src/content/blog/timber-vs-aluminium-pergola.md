The single biggest decision when buying a pergola isn't size or colour — it's **material**. Timber and aluminium look worlds apart, perform differently, and sit at opposite ends of the maintenance curve.

## Timber pergolas

A pressure-treated softwood or hardwood pergola is the classic choice. Costs start around £1,500 fitted for a basic 3 m × 3 m structure.

**Pros**
- Lower upfront cost
- Warm, natural aesthetic that suits period properties and cottage gardens
- Easy to customise on-site — joiners can adapt dimensions to the inch
- Easier to get planning approval in conservation areas

**Cons**
- Needs annual treatment (oil, stain, or paint) to prevent rot and greying
- Structural lifespan of 15–25 years with good maintenance; less without
- Heavier spans may need more support posts

## Aluminium pergolas

Powder-coated aluminium has become the dominant choice for new-build homes and contemporary gardens. Prices start around £4,000 installed.

**Pros**
- Zero rot, no annual treatment — a wipe-down once or twice a year is enough
- Louvered and motorised roof options are only really practical in aluminium
- 25–40 year structural lifespan with minimal degradation
- Available in any RAL colour

**Cons**
- Higher upfront cost
- Can look industrial in very traditional settings
- Less flexibility to adapt mid-build — sizes are largely system-led

## Which should you choose?

| | Timber | Aluminium |
|---|---|---|
| Entry price | £1,500+ | £4,000+ |
| Maintenance | Annual | Minimal |
| Lifespan | 15–25 yrs | 25–40 yrs |
| Louvered roof? | Rarely | Yes |
| Planning in conservation areas | Easier | Can be harder |

If you want a louvered or motorised roof, aluminium is effectively your only option. If budget is tight and you're happy to maintain it, timber is a perfectly good choice that can look stunning in the right garden.
