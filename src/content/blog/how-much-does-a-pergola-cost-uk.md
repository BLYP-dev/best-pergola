Pergola prices in the UK vary considerably depending on material, size, and how mechanised the roof is. Most homeowners spend somewhere between **£3,000 and £15,000** fully installed, but motorised louvered pergolas with full side screens can push well past £20,000.

Before comparing prices, be clear about [how you plan to use the pergola](/blog/what-are-pergolas-used-for/). A simple support for climbing plants and a rain-protected outdoor room sit in very different budget ranges.

## What drives the cost?

Three factors account for the majority of the variation:

1. **Material** — timber is the cheapest entry point; powder-coated aluminium costs more but lasts longer and requires virtually no maintenance.
2. **Roof type** — a fixed polycarbonate or timber-slatted roof is simple to build; a motorised louvered roof with rain sensors involves more engineering and is priced accordingly.
3. **Size and bespoke work** — a 3 m × 3 m standard pergola is a very different proposition from a 6 m × 4 m wrap-around structure with integrated lighting and heating.

## Price ranges by type

| Pergola type | Typical installed cost |
|---|---|
| Wooden pergola, fitted | £1,500 – £4,500 |
| Aluminium, fixed roof | £4,000 – £8,000 |
| Louvered aluminium | £6,000 – £12,000 |
| Motorised louvered | £8,000 – £16,000 |
| Premium with sides & lighting | £12,000 – £25,000+ |

These are indicative ranges. Actual quotes depend on your postcode, the specific installer's day rates, and how complex your groundworks are.

## Groundworks and extras

Don't overlook:

- **Base preparation** — if you don't already have a solid patio, add £1,500–£4,000 for a concrete or porcelain-paved base.
- **Electrical connection** — integrated lighting and motorised roof controls need a qualified electrician. Budget £300–£800.
- **Planning permission** — most pergolas are Permitted Development, but if yours needs a formal application expect another £200–£400 in fees plus surveyor time.

## How to get an accurate quote

The only reliable way to know what your pergola will cost is to get at least two or three quotes from installers who have seen — or at least been given detailed information about — your specific garden.
